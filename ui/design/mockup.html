docz-site on the design system

The reader drawn with @donaldgifford/design-system 0.3.2: its frame, its tokens, and its parts wherever it has one. Where it has none, the part is drawn as docz would propose it, and candidates.html has each proposal on its own. A document keeps its own theme inside the pane, so the pane is drawn in paper, as it is today. This is a hand-drawn sketch to look at; nothing was installed or built.

2. A document, in the package's frame

The repository's navigation becomes sidebar groups under the app's own, so one sidebar carries both. The breadcrumbs move into the top bar. A design's routes take the design type's colour, as they do today: here on the tree's active segment and the type dot. The pane below the details is docz-api's fragment in the document's own theme, with its own tabs, and is not restyled.

Overview

The site's chrome takes repo-guardian's look in light and dark. A document keeps its own theme inside its page, so DESIGN-0025's themes control how a document reads and the shell controls how the app looks.

The reader shows a document as the fragment docz-api renders, so an exported page and the site agree.
$ docz export html DESIGN-0026 --fragment

The sidebar tree is candidate 1 and is the largest thing docz would ask of the package. The collapsed icon rail and the sidebar's keyboard shortcut come with the package's frame, and its mobile sheet replaces the "repo menu" disclosure docz shows on a narrow screen.

3. The same document, in docz's frame

Open question 1, option b: keep the top bar and the per-repository rail, and build them from the package's primitives. Same tokens, same badges, same details card, same tree rows. Readers see less change. The frame itself is one the package does not test, and docz keeps the code for it. The question was resolved for the package's frame (screen 2) on 2026-11-21; this screen stays as the record of what it was compared with.
